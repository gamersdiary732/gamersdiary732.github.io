Gamer’s Diary Games

A one-tap timing game for Android. The ball moves, the zone waits, and the only thing you ever do is choose the moment. Everything else — the shrinking targets, the folded tracks, the ring that has no ends — is that same decision made harder.

Five ways to play

  • ENDLESS — three lives, and a track that keeps changing shape
  • TIMED — thirty seconds, and every dead-centre hit buys a little more
  • CIRCLE — a ring with no ends, a fast arc, and a wall that turns you round
  • DAILY — one board, the same for everybody, new tomorrow
  • CLIMB — tilt to steer, and the ledges do not wait
