PDF Toolkit — Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026 · Published by Gamer's Diary Games

Your documents never leave your phone. Every PDF this app opens, edits, compresses, scans or reads is processed entirely on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else, and there is no server to upload it to.

What the app does with your files

  • Files you choose are read only while a tool is running, and only the files you pick.
  • Results are saved into the app's own private storage on your device, and are visible to you in the app's Recent files list. You can delete them at any time.
  • Text recognition (OCR) and document scanning run on your device using Google's on-device machine-learning models. The page images are not sent anywhere.
  • Your original files are never modified. Every tool writes a new file.
  • Signatures you draw are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are never shared or backed up off the device.
  • We have no account system. You do not register, and we hold no profile about you.

Permissions

The app requests no storage permission. Files reach it only through Android's own file picker, which grants access to the single file you select. The document scanner uses Google Play Services' camera screen, so the app itself never requests camera access. Internet access is used only for the features listed below.

Data collected by third parties

The app itself collects nothing. Two Google services used inside it do collect data, and only in the situations described here.

ServiceWhenWhat it may collect
Google AdMob Only for users on the free version. Nothing is collected once PDF Toolkit Pro is active, because no ads are requested at all. Advertising identifier, approximate (non-precise) location derived from IP address, device and app information, and interaction with ads. Used to select and measure advertising.
Google Play Billing Only if you buy PDF Toolkit Pro. Google processes the purchase. We receive confirmation that a purchase exists for your Google account. We never see your name, card, or address.

Google's own handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and AdMob's in How Google uses data from AdMob.

Your choices about advertising

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form appears before any ad is loaded. You can change your answer later from Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your device's Android settings, under Privacy → Ads.
  • Buying PDF Toolkit Pro removes all advertising, and with it all advertising data collection.

Children

PDF Toolkit is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Files the app has created stay on your device until you delete them, either from within the app or by uninstalling it — uninstalling removes the app's private storage, including any saved signatures. Purchase records are held by Google, not by us.
